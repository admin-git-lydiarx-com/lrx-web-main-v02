A clear division of ownership

What we supply. What you keep.

What LydiaRx supplies

  • The sovereign platform — custody, consent enforcement, metering and provenance.
  • Programme design — phasing, supply chain, laboratory-network model and the reference method.
  • Legal architecture — concession or partnership structure, joint-venture design, supplier terms, and the data-sovereignty and licence framework.
  • Governance design — independent oversight, data-access rules, ethics pathway and published audit.
  • The operator — of the secure environment and the research gateway, under the same controls as every partner.

What the country keeps

  • Every sample and every genome.
  • Ownership of the national pangenome and every dataset derived from it.
  • Access control: a national-majority Data Access Committee decides every use.
  • A defined share of research-access fees and royalties, under audited terms.
  • The capability: the laboratory, the environment, the team and a tested governance standard.

How we engage

Three steps, each verifiable before the next.

Each step is sized so that a government commits nothing it cannot verify before the next step is taken.

Step 1

Feasibility mandate

A non-obligatory blueprint: track selection, entity design, the regulatory map, the funding model and the sponsor-engagement plan. The government receives a document it can test. It commits to nothing.

Step 2

Concept note and MoU

An in-country programme lead and local counsel appointed; the in-country operating company designed; academic and clinical partners identified; a demonstration pilot scoped and costed.

Step 3

Definitive partnership

The concession or partnership agreement, the supplier terms and the data-sovereignty and licence framework executed — and the demonstration pilot delivered and independently audited before any decision to scale.
