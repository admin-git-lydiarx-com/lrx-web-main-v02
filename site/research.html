Why cohorts like those in Pakistan are important

Human knockouts, at national scale.

Homozygous loss-of-function variants are naturally occurring human knockouts. They are among the most valuable starting points in drug-target validation. They show what happens when a gene is switched off in a living person. They occur far more often in consanguineous populations. A governed national cohort, analysed with population-calibrated methods and interpreted with provenance, is a unique resource. Nothing like it exists anywhere else at national scale.
